# A Word — Master Story Template

## Project

**English Beyond Language**

## Recall Name

**A Word**

## Public Series Title

**A Word from the World**

## Format

Instagram Story

- 1080 × 1920 px
- 9:16

## 1. Core Visual Principle

**Structured underneath, alive on the surface.**

Approximate balance:

- 70% editorial clarity
- 30% artistic texture

## 2. Story Flow

**SERIES HEADER**
↓
**MAIN QUOTE / TEXT**
↓
**ATTRIBUTION / SOURCE**
↓
**IMAGE / ARTWORK**

The quote is the primary visual element.

The image supports the meaning.

## 3. Fixed Header

Public title:

**A WORD FROM THE WORLD**

Placement:

- upper center
- small scale
- uppercase
- wide tracking
- Inter
- warm cream / beige

Supporting graphic:

- thin horizontal rule
- small circular EBL-style marker
- restrained geometry

## 4. Logo

Use the official **English Beyond Language** logo.

Placement:

**top-right**

Story-specific behavior:

- relatively large
- clearly visible
- integrated into the composition
- never distorted
- never recolored
- preserve original proportions

The logo acts as part of the visual architecture rather than a small watermark.

## 5. Quote Area

Placement:

**upper-middle / central reading field**

Typography:

**Cormorant Garamond**

Characteristics:

- large literary serif
- tight but readable line spacing
- left aligned
- strong editorial line breaks
- generous surrounding negative space

### Color hierarchy

Base text:

**warm cream**

Semantic emphasis:

**burnt orange**

Only key words or phrases should be highlighted.

Do not color entire paragraphs.

## 6. Quotation Marks

Large opening and closing quotation marks may function as graphic elements.

Color:

**burnt orange**

Placement:

- opening mark near the beginning of the quote block
- closing mark near the lower/right edge of the quote

They should support composition, not compete with the text.

## 7. Divider

Below the quote:

- thin horizontal rule
- small circular marker
- orange / cream detail

Purpose:

separate the quote from attribution while maintaining the EBL geometric language.

## 8. Attribution

Hierarchy:

**Author / inspiration**
**Source / title / date**

Typography:

Cormorant Garamond Italic or restrained serif.

Scale:

small and subordinate.

Color:

warm beige / cream.

Placement:

directly below the quote divider.

## 9. Image Field

Placement:

**lower 45–50% of the Story**

Image types:

- photography
- artwork
- archival imagery
- culturally meaningful visual material

Preferred treatment:

- monochrome
- sepia
- muted duotone
- restrained cinematic contrast

### Critical transition rule

The image should **fade softly into the dark upper field**.

Avoid a hard rectangular boundary.

Use:

**Image → atmospheric gradient → black quote field**

## 10. Geometric Frame

Geometry enters from the edges rather than sitting in the center.

### Top-left

More active cluster:

- quarter circles
- rectangles
- cream blocks
- muted teal
- burnt orange
- dark shapes

### Left side

- thin vertical rule
- small orange circle
- restrained modular alignment

### Right side

More restrained:

- thin vertical rule
- partial arc
- small rectangular interruption
- subtle geometric support

### Bottom

Geometry becomes denser again.

Use:

- circles
- quarter circles
- blocks
- arcs
- modular combinations

Geometric elements may overlap the image.

## 11. Color System

### Base

Deep charcoal / black

### Primary text

Warm cream

### Secondary text

Warm beige

### Accent

Burnt orange / rust

### Supporting color

Muted teal / desaturated green

### Optional support

Warm brown / muted ochre

## 12. Typography

### Inter

Use for:

- series title
- functional information
- labels
- metadata

### Cormorant Garamond

Use for:

- quotation
- literary emphasis
- attribution
- cultural/source information

## 13. Texture

Apply subtle tactile texture across:

- background
- colored geometry
- photographic treatment

Preferred qualities:

- paper grain
- archival surface
- subtle noise
- restrained material texture

Avoid:

- heavy grunge
- fake scratches
- excessive vintage effects

## 14. Fixed Elements

These stay consistent:

- public title
- EBL logo
- overall hierarchy
- typography roles
- palette
- quote-first structure
- geometric DNA
- image position
- image-to-black fade
- editorial tone
- texture philosophy

## 15. Variable Elements

These change for each piece:

- exact quote
- highlighted words
- author
- source
- date
- image / artwork
- crop
- mood
- degree of sepia / monochrome treatment
- source-specific visual references

## 16. Content Rule

Exact supplied source text must remain unchanged.

**Never paraphrase the quotation.**

## 17. QC

Before approval check:

- [ ] Correct EBL logo used
- [ ] Overall result feels editorial, cultural, and recognizably EBL
- [ ] Quote is visually dominant
- [ ] Exact text preserved
- [ ] Attribution accurate
- [ ] Orange emphasis is selective
- [ ] Image supports the meaning
- [ ] Image fades naturally into black
- [ ] Geometry frames rather than distracts
- [ ] Texture remains subtle
- [ ] Strong readability
- [ ] Public title reads exactly **A Word from the World**

## Master Rule

The template should remain recognizable even when the quote, source, image, and mood completely change.

**Structure stays constant.**
**Meaning changes.**
