# VIS-001 — Composition Analysis

## Reference Observations

### OR-01 Sam Café
- Overall structure: compact emblem centered within a larger field.
- Spatial organization: one dominant circular container subdivided into smaller modules.
- Hierarchy: outer circle first, internal division second, compact text/icons third.
- Balance: near-symmetrical container with internally asymmetric content distribution.
- Negative space: generous external kraft field; dense information concentrated inside the mark.
- Rhythm: repeated quadrant logic and thin linear dividers.
- Eye path: container → crossing lines → local text/icon modules.

### OR-03 / OR-04 Studio Melli
- Overall structure: modular rectangular fields with strong type-led anchors.
- Spatial organization: asymmetric editorial blocks rather than a single centered emblem.
- Hierarchy: dominant typographic gesture → secondary text/blocks → micro detail.
- Alignment: visible grid discipline with controlled breaks.
- Negative space: active rather than leftover; gaps separate modules and amplify type.
- Rhythm: repeated rectangular proportions, typographic scale shifts, and edge alignments.
- Eye path: high-contrast typographic anchor → neighboring block → supporting information.

## Comparative Analysis

### Recurring Principles
1. Strong containment or field logic improves recognition.
2. A limited number of dominant structural moves carry the composition.
3. Negative space is functional and hierarchy-bearing.
4. Dense detail works best when confined to smaller zones.

### Major Difference
Sam relies on a compact emblematic center; Studio Melli relies on an editorial field system.

### Extracted Composition DNA
- Use one primary structural gesture per composition.
- Build recognition through consistent spacing and field relationships, not decorative accumulation.
- Preserve a high ratio of calm negative space around dense information.
- Use asymmetric editorial layouts for applications, while allowing a compact mark for small-scale identity.
- Do not use a circular quadrant device or directly reproduce any observed reference composition.

### Provenance / Confidence
- `VIS-COMP-01`: functional negative space — OR-01/03/04 — High.
- `VIS-COMP-02`: concentrated density plus open field — OR-01/03/04 — High.
- `VIS-COMP-03`: modular editorial application system — OR-03/04 — High.

`PASS`

- Preserve complete functionality in black/white or one-ink production.

# VDNA-001 — Visual DNA Extraction

## Core Visual Concept
**The Interval** — a calm, intentional band of negative space that acts as Noura’s structural signature: a visual pause between scripts, information blocks, materials, and moments.

This is not a literal pause icon. It is a controlled spacing rule and open structural gap.

## Visual Character
Quiet, precise, bilingual, tactile, urban, contemporary, observant, non-luxury, non-nostalgic.

## Composition DNA
- One dominant alignment system.
- Large quiet field around a concentrated information zone.
- A recurring narrow-to-medium open interval interrupts or separates modules.
- Asymmetry is allowed but must be visibly grid-controlled.
- Small-format identity collapses to a compact wordmark/interval lockup.

Sources: `VIS-001`.

## Color DNA
- Warm mineral light neutral foundation.
- Soot/ink dark neutral for primary type and mark.
- One muted earth/mineral accent, low-to-medium saturation, used selectively.
- Monochrome equivalent always valid.

Sources: `VIS-002`, fixture muted-color requirement.

## Shape & Form DNA
- Rectangular fields, bars, open frames, controlled notches/cuts.
- The gap/interval is more important than any decorative shape.
- No coffee-icon vocabulary in the core mark.

Sources: `VIS-003`.

## Texture & Material DNA
- Real tactility before simulated texture.
- Uncoated paper, economical labels, stamps, paint, brushed/fabricated signage, matte digital rendering.
- Controlled material imperfection is acceptable; artificial grunge is not.

Sources: `VIS-004`, production decisions D-07/D-08.

## Typography & Graphic DNA
- Persian and Latin have equal semantic authority.
- Optical balance beats forced formal similarity.
- Display type may be large and spatial; support copy stays editorial and restrained.
- Rules/blocks/intervals provide graphic grammar.

Sources: `VIS-005`, D-04, D-07.

## Lighting & Atmosphere DNA
- Soft, material-readable, calm and inhabited.
- Moderate contrast.
- No default dark-industrial mood or glossy luxury staging.

Sources: `VIS-006`.

## Visual Grammar
1. IF Persian and Latin appear together, THEN separate or relate them with the same interval unit rather than treating one as a subtitle.
2. IF a layout becomes dense, THEN preserve at least one uninterrupted quiet field large enough to reset hierarchy.
3. IF color is added, THEN use the accent only for orientation/emphasis and preserve monochrome equivalence.
4. IF tactility is desired, THEN use substrate/material/print behavior before adding digital noise.
5. IF a graphic form is added, THEN it must reinforce alignment, interval, or information hierarchy.
6. IF an application is small, THEN remove secondary graphic elements before reducing the core wordmark below recognition threshold.
7. IF an Iranian cultural signal is needed, THEN prioritize language, bilingual rhythm, material context, and present-tense urban cues before historical motifs.

## Core / Supporting / Flexible / Avoid

| Level | Rules |
|---|---|
| Core | Bilingual equality; interval signature; strong negative space; one-color viability; anti-stereotype rule; small-size recognition. |
| Supporting | Warm mineral neutrals; muted earth accent; tactile uncoated materials; editorial grid; soft material-readable atmosphere. |
| Flexible | Exact accent hue; image crop; degree of asymmetry; application-specific frame dimensions; controlled secondary neutral. |
| Avoid | Circular quadrant café badge; coffee-bean/cup symbols as core identity; ornamental Persian motifs; pseudo-calligraphy; black-luxury styling; bright palettes; decorative grunge. |

## Anti-DNA
- Not generic global third-wave monochrome minimalism.
- Not “Persian luxury.”
- Not heritage pastiche.
- Not a coffee-bean monogram.
- Not Persian text appended as translation beneath a Latin master brand.
- Not a noisy craft aesthetic.

## One-Sentence Visual DNA
**Noura is a bilingual editorial identity built from quiet intervals, mineral restraint, real tactility, and present-tense Tehran context.**

# ART-001 — Creative Concept Generation

## Creative Problem
Create a recognizable coffee identity that feels calm, contemporary, and Iranian without collapsing into generic specialty-coffee minimalism or stereotypical Persian visual language.

## Concept C1 — The Interval
- Central idea: the brand is a deliberate pause in a dense city day.
- Mechanism: a consistent band of negative space divides/links Persian and Latin, content blocks, packaging labels, and environmental graphics.
- Metaphor: pause through space, not an icon.
- Perception: calm, intentional, intelligent, contemporary.
- Composition: editorial field with one signature void.
- Shape: open frames, interrupted bars, rectangular modules.
- Color: mineral neutral base + muted clay accent.
- Material: uncoated paper, stamps/labels, matte paint/fabrication.
- Typography: balanced `نورا` / `NOURA`, neither subordinate.
- Atmosphere: soft daylight, quiet material presence.
- Complexity: Low–Medium.
- Risk: gap could become generic if spacing rules are not distinctive enough.

## Concept C2 — Dual Current
- Central idea: Persian and Latin move as two parallel currents through one system.
- Mechanism: paired baselines and alternating reading directions generate layouts.
- Perception: bilingual, dynamic, culturally situated.
- Composition: two coordinated typographic bands.
- Shape: parallel lines and paired blocks.
- Color: neutral pair with one shared accent.
- Material: flat print with occasional tactile separators.
- Complexity: Medium.
- Risk: can become visually busy and over-emphasize bilingual technique over café character.

## Concept C3 — Urban Ledger
- Central idea: Noura documents the daily city ritual with the clarity of an editorial ledger.
- Mechanism: date/time/origin/menu information becomes a systematic grid language.
- Perception: intelligent, precise, contemporary, useful.
- Composition: modular information grid, tabular rhythm, strong hierarchy.
- Shape: rule lines, labels, columns, open frames.
- Color: almost monochrome with coded accent.
- Material: receipt/menu paper, labels, stamps.
- Complexity: Low.
- Risk: can become too informational/clinical and under-deliver emotional warmth.

## Concept C4 — Material Ritual
- Central idea: identity emerges through repeated physical traces of coffee ritual.
- Mechanism: stamp pressure, paper grain, cup/label contact, and subtle imperfect registration become system evidence.
- Perception: tactile, human, grounded.
- Composition: quiet fields with isolated physical impressions.
- Shape: restrained blocks and stamp areas.
- Color: earthy monochrome/duotone.
- Material: strongest route for paper/packaging tactility.
- Complexity: Medium.
- Risk: can drift into generic craft branding if the bilingual/interval system is weak.

## Diversity Check

| Concept | Central Idea | Mechanism | Emotional Territory | Main Difference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Pause | Negative-space interval | Calm/attentive | Space itself is signature. |
| C2 | Two scripts, one rhythm | Paired typographic currents | Energetic/intellectual | Bilingual motion is signature. |
| C3 | Daily urban record | Editorial information grid | Precise/observant | Information structure is signature. |
| C4 | Physical ritual | Material trace/impression | Warm/tactile | Production material is signature. |

# ART-003 — Approved Art Direction

## Creative Principle
**Make room for attention.** Every application should visibly create a controlled interval where information or material can breathe.

## Visual Character
Calm, exact, bilingual, tactile, urban, intelligent, non-precious.

## Identity Lockup
- Primary lockup uses Persian `نورا` and Latin `NOURA` as equal brand names.
- The scripts may sit horizontally or vertically depending on application, but the same interval unit separates/relates them.
- The interval is a blank structural band, not a drawn pause icon.
- A compact one-script lockup may be used only where the other script is genuinely unavailable or illegible at required size; bilingual form remains default.

## Composition Direction
- Use editorial grids with a dominant quiet field.
- Place the main brand block off-center in larger applications to avoid conventional centered-badge café styling.
- Repeat the interval as a spacing unit between brand, product, origin, date, or navigation information.
- Use dense micro-information only inside clearly bounded zones.

## Color Direction
Primary family:
- warm limestone / bone field;
- soot / ink typography;
- muted dusty brick / oxidized clay accent.

Optional environmental neutral:
- desaturated olive-gray or concrete gray.

Rules:
- Accent should occupy a minority of the field.
- One-color black, dark brown-black, or reversed white execution must remain complete.
- No gradients are required for identity recognition.

## Shape & Form Direction
- Open rectangular frames.
- Interrupted bars/rules.
- Controlled rectangular notches.
- No circles as primary identity containers.
- No coffee beans, cups, steam, roasters, mountains, Persian arches, tile lattices, or paisley in the core mark.

## Texture & Material Direction
- Uncoated or lightly textured paper.
- Economical labels and stamps allowed.
- Painted/cut/fabricated signage with broad simple forms.
- Matte surfaces preferred.
- Texture must arise from material or image context; do not overlay generic distressed effects.

## Typography & Graphic Direction
- Select a verified contemporary Persian typeface and a Latin companion with compatible optical weight, density, and vertical rhythm.
- Persian and Latin do not need identical skeletons.
- Use generous tracking/spacing in Latin display settings only where legibility remains natural.
- Persian display setting should preserve joining behavior and natural word shape.
- Support typography uses simple editorial scale levels: brand / title / information / micro.
- Graphic support: interval, rule line, rectangular field, small index marks.

## Lighting & Atmosphere
- Soft natural window light or soft mixed ambient light.
- Moderate contrast; material detail remains visible.
- Avoid black-background luxury photography, harsh spotlights, excessive steam, and hyper-glossy CGI.

## Image Treatment
- Architectural/material close-ups and human-scale café context preferred over hero shots of beans.
- Crops may be tight and observational.
- People, if shown, should appear naturally present rather than staged as lifestyle advertising.
- Photography supports atmosphere; typography/system remains the identity anchor.

## Visual Hierarchy
1. Brand/script relationship.
2. Primary content/product information.
3. Interval/field structure.
4. Material/image context.
5. Micro metadata.

## Rules of Combination
1. Never use all secondary devices at once.
2. Every layout must contain either the bilingual lockup or a valid small-scale identity mark.
3. Every layout must contain at least one intentional quiet interval.
4. If imagery is visually dense, typography and graphics become simpler.
5. If typography is large/expressive, imagery becomes quieter or disappears.
6. Accent color never competes with both large typography and a strong image simultaneously.

## DO
- Let space carry meaning.
- Balance Persian/Latin optically.
- Use material economy intentionally.
- Keep core identity viable in one color.
- Test at small size and storefront scale.

## DON'T
- Add a coffee icon because the category is coffee.
- Attach Persian as a translation after the Latin brand.
- Use ornamental calligraphy imitation.
- Use dark industrial styling as the entire brand personality.
- Use bright accent stacks or luxury metallic effects as defaults.

## Fixed / Controlled / Flexible

| Type | Items |
|---|---|
| Fixed | Name, bilingual equality, interval concept, anti-stereotype constraints, one-color viability, small-size recognition. |
| Controlled | Accent hue, interval width, frame dimensions, image crop, degree of asymmetry. |
| Flexible | Application-specific metadata, image subject, material stock within matte/economical family, secondary neutral. |

## Production Translation
- Cup: single wrap label or direct one-color mark with interval separating product metadata.
- Coffee bag: large quiet substrate field, off-center bilingual lockup, small origin/roast information block.
- Storefront: broad fabricated or painted `نورا / NOURA` lockup with visible interval; no fine line dependency.
- Menu: Urban Ledger behavior can support prices/categories, but brand interval remains dominant.
- Instagram: reusable field/grid templates; typography first; image optional.
- Website: same interval unit becomes spacing/token logic in navigation and content modules.
