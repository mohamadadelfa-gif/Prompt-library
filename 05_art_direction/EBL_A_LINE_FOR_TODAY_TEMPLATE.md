# English Beyond Language — “A Line for Today” Story Template

## Status
Current working template. This may evolve in future iterations.

## Purpose
Create a recognizable Instagram Story series for **English Beyond Language (EBL)** where the quote is the primary focus and the visual context supports the meaning of the quote.

## Core Layout

### 1. Series Header
- Text: **A LINE FOR TODAY**
- Position: **top center**
- Use only once in the composition.
- Keep it secondary to the quote.

### 2. Brand Logo
- Position: **top right**
- Use the **English Beyond Language** logo/series mark.
- Do not duplicate “A Line for Today” inside or behind the logo if the series header already appears at the top center.
- Logo must remain clearly legible but should not compete with the quote.

### 3. Quote Area
- Position: **upper-middle / central area**
- This is the dominant visual hierarchy.
- Allocate generous negative space around the quote.
- Typography may adapt to the literary/historical/cultural context of each quote, but readability must remain the priority.
- Quote text must be clearly readable at Instagram Story viewing size.

### 4. Author + Source
- Position: **directly below the quote**
- Author should be clearly visible.
- Source/work title may be smaller than the author but must remain readable.
- Verify spelling and attribution during QC.

### 5. Contextual Image
- Role: **supporting context, not the primary focus**
- Prefer imagery that visually reinforces the meaning/action of the quote.
- The image can occupy the lower and background areas while leaving the quote area readable.
- Important contextual action should remain visually clear (for example, laughter in the Mark Twain story).

### 6. EBL Graphic Layer
- Use a **thin, restrained geometric layer** inspired by the EBL visual system and Paul Klee-like geometric abstraction.
- Typical elements: circles, arches, rectangular blocks, fine lines, dots, grids, cropped geometric forms.
- Keep these elements mostly near edges/corners so they frame the content rather than compete with it.
- Preferred palette: dark/black base, warm cream, burnt orange/rust, muted teal/green, ochre/gold accents.

## Visual Hierarchy

1. **Quote** — primary focus
2. **Author / source** — attribution
3. **Contextual image/action** — meaning support
4. **A LINE FOR TODAY** — series identifier
5. **EBL logo** — brand identifier
6. **Geometric decoration** — supporting layer

## Composition Logic

**Top center:** A LINE FOR TODAY  
**Top right:** EBL logo  
**Upper-middle / center:** large quote  
**Below quote:** author + source  
**Lower/background:** contextual image/action  
**Edges/corners:** restrained EBL geometric language

## Remove / Avoid

- Do not add extra footer branding such as “Keyvan • English Beyond Language” unless explicitly requested.
- Do not add side keyword lists such as “Words, Books, Ideas, People, Culture.”
- Do not repeat “A Line for Today.”
- Do not let decorative shapes overpower the quote.
- Do not redesign freely when editing an approved template; preserve established placement unless explicitly asked to change it.

## Heavy QC

Before final approval, check:

- Exact quote spelling
- Author spelling
- Source/work title accuracy
- Quote readability at Story size
- Author/source readability
- Strong contrast between text and background
- Logo fidelity and placement
- “A LINE FOR TODAY” appears only once
- No unwanted duplicate branding
- Contextual image clearly supports the quote
- Important action in the image is easy to perceive
- Geometric layer remains subtle
- Existing approved composition is preserved when only a local edit is requested

## Current Design Principle

> The template stays consistent, while the photograph, quote typography, and contextual details can change from story to story.

This creates a recognizable **series system** instead of a one-off poster.
