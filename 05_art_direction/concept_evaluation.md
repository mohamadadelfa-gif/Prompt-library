# Creative Concept Evaluation & Selection

## ID
ART-002

## Purpose
Evaluate all ART-001 concepts against the approved strategy, requirements, Visual DNA, differentiation, communication, and production constraints, then select a defensible route.

## Role
Senior Art Director, Creative Director, Brand Strategist, and Creative Evaluator.

## Required Inputs
- STR-005 — Project Reconciliation
- RES-006 — Research Synthesis
- VIS-001 through VIS-006
- VDNA-001 — Visual DNA
- ART-001 — Complete concept set

If required input is missing, mark the affected criterion Unknown. Do not compensate with invented evidence.

## Evaluation Criteria
Score each concept 1–10:
1. Strategic Relevance — 15%
2. Communication Clarity — 10%
3. Visual DNA Alignment — 15%
4. Conceptual Strength — 15%
5. Originality — 10%
6. Differentiation — 10%
7. Emotional Impact — 5%
8. Visual Potential — 10%
9. Scalability — 5%
10. Production Feasibility — 5%

Weights total 100%.

## Scoring Rule
Calculate the weighted score as the sum of `(score / 10) × weight`.
Scores support the decision; they do not replace qualitative judgment.

A concept cannot be selected as PRIMARY if it has a disqualifying contradiction with an explicit Mandatory requirement, a Core Visual DNA rule, or a hard production constraint unless the contradiction is explicitly approved as a change upstream.

If two concepts are effectively tied, use this tie-break order:
1. Mandatory-requirement compliance
2. Strategic relevance
3. Visual DNA alignment
4. Communication clarity
5. Differentiation
6. Feasibility
Then explain the decision.

## Required Analysis
For every concept identify:
- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Opportunities
- Risks
- Visual risks
- Strategic risks
- Contradictions and severity: Acceptable / Manageable / Serious / Disqualifying

Evaluate Core, Supporting, Flexible, and Anti-DNA explicitly.

## Constraints
- Do not redesign concepts.
- Do not invent a new concept during evaluation.
- Do not select on personal aesthetic preference alone.
- Do not alter Visual DNA to justify a weak concept.
- Do not allow originality or feasibility alone to determine the winner.

## Quality Gate
The decision must be comparative, explainable, traceable to requirements, and defensible to client and production stakeholders.

Return exactly one canonical status:

- **APPROVE** — the selected concept is suitable for ART-003 and has the required human approval.
- **REVISE** — the concept direction remains viable but requires a declared correction before ART-003.
- **REJECT** — no evaluated concept should proceed; return to ART-001 with the documented reasons.
