# Creative Concept Generation

## ID
ART-001

## Purpose
Generate 3–5 genuinely different creative routes from the approved strategy and Visual DNA. Explore concepts without changing the established strategic or visual foundation.

## Role
Senior Art Director, Creative Director, Concept Designer, and Visual Strategist.

## Required Inputs
- STR-005 — Project Reconciliation
- RES-006 — Research Synthesis
- VIS-001 through VIS-006 — Visual Analysis
- VDNA-001 — Visual DNA

If a required input is missing, mark the affected area Unknown. Do not invent it.

## Core Rules
- Concepts must solve the same project problem through meaningfully different central ideas.
- Variation by color, font, background, or minor layout is not conceptual diversity.
- Visual DNA is a constraint system, not a moodboard to copy.
- Do not introduce unsupported requirements or references.
- Do not generate final artwork or image-generation prompts.

## Process
### 1. Define the Creative Problem
Separate communication, visual, emotional, and functional problems where applicable.

### 2. Identify Opportunities
Use evidence-supported opportunities from research and Visual DNA.

### 3. Generate Concepts
Create 3–5 concepts with different conceptual mechanisms, not superficial styling differences.

### 4. Test Each Concept Against Visual DNA
For every concept identify how it uses Core, Supporting, Flexible, and Avoid principles. If it intentionally challenges a principle, explain the controlled exception.

## Concept Structure
For each concept provide:
- Concept ID
- Concept Name
- Central Idea
- Conceptual Mechanism
- Visual Metaphor, or “No dominant metaphor”
- Intended Audience Perception
- Narrative / Communication Mechanism
- Composition Direction
- Shape Direction
- Color Direction
- Texture / Material Direction
- Typography / Graphic Direction
- Lighting / Atmosphere Direction
- Visual DNA Alignment
- Differentiation from other concepts
- Strengths
- Risks
- Production Complexity: Low / Medium / High

## Diversity Check
| Concept | Central Idea | Mechanism | Emotional Territory | Visual Territory | Main Difference |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Reject or regenerate concepts that are effectively variations of one route.

## Recommendation
Identify:
- Strongest strategic concept
- Most differentiated concept
- Most innovative concept
- Safest concept
- Most expandable concept

Do not select the final winner solely on aesthetics. Recommend one primary and one alternative route, with reasons.

## Handoff to ART-002
The handoff must contain the complete concept set, comparison matrix, risks, and Visual DNA compliance so ART-002 can evaluate without reconstructing prior reasoning.

## Quality Gate
Concepts pass only if they are strategically relevant, conceptually distinct, explainable, developable, production-aware, and compatible with the Visual DNA.
