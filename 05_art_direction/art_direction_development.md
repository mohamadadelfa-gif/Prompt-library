# Art Direction Development

## ID
ART-003

## Purpose
Transform the selected ART-002 concept into an executable visual decision system without changing the concept or Visual DNA.

## Role
Senior Art Director, Creative Director, Visual Identity Designer, and Production Art Director.

## Required Inputs
- STR-005 — Project Reconciliation
- RES-006 — Research Synthesis
- VIS-001 through VIS-006
- VDNA-001 — Visual DNA
- ART-002 — Selected concept and Improvement Brief

If required input is missing, mark the affected area UNKNOWN. Do not invent it.

## Preconditions
ART-002 must have an APPROVE decision and the required human approval record. If not, return BLOCKED.

## Core Principle
Art Direction defines rules for making consistent visual decisions. It is not a description of one finished image and it is not a generation prompt.

## Instructions
Translate the selected concept into:
1. Creative Principle
2. Visual Character
3. Composition Direction
4. Color Direction
5. Shape & Form Direction
6. Texture & Material Direction
7. Typography & Graphic Direction
8. Lighting & Atmosphere Direction
9. Image Treatment
10. Visual Hierarchy
11. Rules of Combination
12. DO / DON'T
13. Fixed / Controlled / Flexible system

Create project-specific IF → THEN rules. Use precise, observable language. Every major rule must remain compatible with Visual DNA.

## Output Contract
- Creative Principle
- Visual Character
- Composition Direction
- Color Direction
- Shape & Form Direction
- Texture & Material Direction
- Typography & Graphic Direction
- Lighting & Atmosphere
- Image Treatment
- Visual Hierarchy
- Rules of Combination
- DO / DON'T
- Fixed / Controlled / Flexible
- Production Translation
- Art Direction Summary

## Traceability
| Art Direction Rule | Source: Concept / Visual DNA / Requirement | Status |
|---|---|---|

Status values: Preserved, Translated, Controlled Exception. A Controlled Exception requires explicit justification.

## Gate Decision
Return exactly one canonical status:
- APPROVE — Art Direction is complete, traceable, executable, and ready for GEN-001.
- REVISE — direction is viable but incomplete or internally inconsistent.
- BLOCKED — required approval, evidence, or upstream input is missing.

Human approval is required before GEN-001 may execute.

## Handoff to GEN-001
Include approved visual rules, critical requirements, preservation locks, flexible parameters, forbidden characteristics, unresolved production questions, provenance, confidence, and gate status.

## Constraints
- Do not create final artwork.
- Do not generate image-generation prompts.
- Do not redesign the selected concept.
- Do not contradict Visual DNA without explicit justification.
- Do not introduce unrelated visual styles.
- Do not rely on vague adjectives.

## Quality Gate
The Art Direction must be strategically aligned, executable, specific, coherent, flexible, scalable, production-aware, and traceable.
