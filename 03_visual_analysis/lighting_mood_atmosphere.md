# Lighting, Mood & Atmosphere Analysis

## ID
VIS-006

## Purpose
Analyze lighting, mood, atmosphere, and environmental qualities of selected visual references and extract transferable principles for Visual DNA.

## Role
Senior Art Director, Cinematographer, Photographer, Lighting Designer, and Visual Analyst.

## Required Inputs
- STR-005 — Project Reconciliation
- RES-005 — Visual Reference Research
- RES-006 — Research Synthesis
- Selected visual references approved for analysis
- VIS-001 — Composition Analysis
- VIS-002 — Color Analysis
- VIS-003 — Shape & Form Analysis
- VIS-004 — Texture & Material Analysis
- VIS-005 — Typography & Graphic Language Analysis

## Task Boundary
Analyze observable lighting structure, atmosphere, emotional effects, and environmental qualities. Do not prescribe the final lighting design or Art Direction.

## Method
Analyze light source/direction/quality/intensity/distribution, hard/soft and direct/diffused light, natural/artificial characteristics, shadow and highlight behavior, contrast/tonal range, exposure, atmospheric depth, haze/fog/smoke/dust, spatial clarity, perceived temperature, emotional tone, sense of place/time, energy, tension, intimacy, and realism/abstraction.

Do not assign emotional meaning without explaining the observable visual mechanisms supporting it. Do not invent a physical light source that cannot be inferred.

## Output Contract
### Reference Analysis
- Light Source / Direction / Quality / Intensity / Distribution
- Hard / Soft and Direct / Diffused Characteristics
- Natural / Artificial Characteristics
- Lighting Structure
- Shadow Characteristics
- Highlight Characteristics
- Contrast / Tonal Range / Exposure
- Atmospheric Characteristics / Spatial Depth
- Environmental Conditions
- Emotional Tone with visual evidence
- Sense of Place / Time
- Energy / Tension / Intimacy
- Realism / Abstraction

### Comparative Analysis
- Recurring Lighting Principles
- Shared Atmospheric Characteristics
- Shared Emotional Qualities
- Major Differences
- Complementary Characteristics
- Contradictions
- Unique Characteristics

### Extracted Lighting & Atmosphere DNA
Describe reusable lighting/atmospheric principles rather than a specific scene.

### Provenance / Confidence
Every major derived principle must reference the source observation/reference and use Low / Medium / High confidence.

### Gate Decision
Return exactly one canonical status:
- PASS
- CONDITIONAL
- BLOCKED

BLOCKED when required references or evidence are missing or materially ambiguous.

## Handoff
Pass the Lighting/Mood/Atmosphere Analysis package to VDNA-001 with source IDs, observations, derived principles, unknowns, and blockers.

## Constraints
- Do not confuse color with lighting.
- Do not confuse darkness with low-key lighting without analyzing structure.
- Do not assign emotion without evidence.
- Do not copy a lighting setup literally.
- Focus on transferable principles.

## Quality Gate
The analysis must be observable, technically informed, specific, comparative, transferable, and useful for Visual DNA extraction.
