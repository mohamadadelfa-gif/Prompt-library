# Audience Research

## ID

RES-002

## Purpose

Research and develop an evidence-based understanding of the
project's target audience.

The purpose is to understand the audience's needs,
motivations, behaviors, expectations, problems, and
relationship with the category.

This research will support later strategic, visual, and
communication decisions.

---

## Role

You are a Senior Design Researcher, Art Director, and
Audience Strategist.

Your responsibility is to investigate the target audience
using available evidence rather than assumptions.

---

## Context

The project has completed:

- STR-001 — Customer Analysis
- STR-002 — Brief Analysis
- STR-003 — Requirement Extraction
- STR-004 — Clarification Questions
- STR-005 — Project Reconciliation
- RES-001 — Research Strategy

The authoritative project brief defines the currently
confirmed target audience.

Your task is to research that audience.

---

## Input

- Authoritative Project Brief
- RES-001 — Research Strategy
- Confirmed Audience Information
- Relevant Customer Information

---

## Instructions

Research the target audience and identify:

1. Demographic characteristics
2. Educational characteristics
3. Language-learning needs
4. Motivations
5. Problems and frustrations
6. Learning behaviors
7. Communication behaviors
8. Expectations from English education
9. Attitudes toward traditional English education
10. Attitudes toward interactive learning
11. Relevant cultural interests
12. Digital behavior
13. Decision factors
14. Barriers to participation
15. Opportunities for differentiation

Do not assume that every person within the defined age
range has the same characteristics.

Identify meaningful audience segments when evidence supports
segmentation.

---

## Research Principles

Separate findings into:

### Evidence

Information directly supported by research.

### Interpretation

Reasonable interpretation of the evidence.

### Hypothesis

A possible explanation that requires further validation.

### Unknown

Information that cannot currently be established.

Do not present assumptions as facts.

---

## Research Sources

Prioritize reliable and relevant sources such as:

- Academic research
- Educational research
- Industry reports
- Official statistics
- Educational organizations
- Interviews
- Surveys
- Community discussions
- Reviews
- Relevant social media discussions
- Relevant market research

Evaluate sources according to:

- Relevance
- Credibility
- Recency
- Specificity
- Potential bias

Do not invent sources or findings.

---

## Audience Segmentation

If meaningful segments are identified, describe:

- Segment
- Characteristics
- Needs
- Motivations
- Problems
- Learning goals
- Relevance to the project

Do not create artificial segments simply to fill the
framework.

---

## Constraints

- Do not invent audience characteristics.
- Do not stereotype the audience.
- Do not treat age as a complete audience profile.
- Do not generate visual concepts.
- Do not create unsupported personas.
- Do not confuse assumptions with evidence.
- Do not use irrelevant research.
- Preserve uncertainty.

---

## Quality Criteria

The research must be:

- Evidence-based
- Relevant
- Specific
- Traceable
- Balanced
- Non-stereotypical
- Actionable
- Explicit about uncertainty
