# Audience Research

## ID
RES-002

## Purpose
Research and develop an evidence-based understanding of the project's target audience, including relevant needs, motivations, behaviors, expectations, barriers, decision factors, and contextual characteristics.

## Role
Senior Design Researcher, Art Director, and Audience Strategist.

## Required Inputs
- STR-005 — Project Reconciliation
- RES-001 — Research Strategy
- Confirmed Audience Information
- Relevant Customer Information

If required input is unavailable, mark it UNKNOWN. Do not reconstruct missing evidence.

## Task Boundary
Research the established audience for the actual project domain. Do not invent personas, define final positioning, or generate creative concepts.

## Research Areas
Investigate only areas relevant to the established project, such as:
1. Demographic/contextual characteristics when decision-relevant
2. Needs and problems
3. Motivations
4. Behaviors and habits
5. Communication behavior
6. Expectations
7. Decision factors
8. Barriers to participation or adoption
9. Digital/contextual behavior
10. Cultural or contextual factors when relevant

Never assume that everyone in a demographic group behaves identically.

## Evidence Rules
Separate:
- Evidence — directly supported finding
- Observation — documented pattern
- Interpretation — reasoned conclusion
- Hypothesis — requires further validation
- Unknown — cannot currently be established

Evaluate source relevance, credibility, recency, specificity, and potential bias. Do not invent sources or findings.

## Segmentation
Create segments only when evidence supports meaningful differences in needs, motivations, behaviors, or decision factors. Otherwise state that no evidence-based segmentation was identified.

## Provenance / Confidence
Every material finding must cite its evidence/source and use Low / Medium / High confidence. Unsupported demographic claims are not acceptable.

## Gate Decision
Return exactly one canonical status:
- PASS
- CONDITIONAL
- BLOCKED

## Handoff
Pass evidence-backed audience findings, segments if supported, unknowns, hypotheses, limitations, and confidence to RES-003 and RES-004.

## Constraints
- Do not invent audience characteristics.
- Do not stereotype.
- Do not use demographics as a substitute for evidence.
- Do not create unsupported personas.
- Do not generate visual concepts.
- Preserve uncertainty.

## Quality Criteria
Evidence-based, relevant, specific, traceable, balanced, non-stereotypical, actionable, and explicit about uncertainty.
