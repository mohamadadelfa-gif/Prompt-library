# Research Strategy

## ID
RES-001

## Purpose
Determine what research is necessary before visual analysis and art direction begin.

## Role
Senior Art Director, Design Researcher, and Strategist.

## Required Inputs
- STR-005 — Authoritative Project Reconciliation
- Confirmed Requirements
- Known Audience
- Known Constraints
- Known Uncertainties
- Existing Customer References

## Task Boundary
Define research questions, source types, priorities, boundaries, sequence, expected outputs, and completion criteria. Do not perform the research.

## Research Areas
Investigate only areas relevant to the established project domain. Possible areas include:
1. Audience
2. Market / Category
3. Competitors / Alternatives
4. Industry / Domain Context
5. Cultural / Social Context
6. Communication Context
7. Existing Visual Landscape
8. Trends / Contemporary Context
9. Differentiation Research

Do not force every project into every area.

## Research Question Rules
Questions must be specific, answerable, decision-linked, non-leading, non-redundant, and grounded in the authoritative brief. Do not research topics that cannot influence a project decision.

## Source Strategy
Possible source types include official organizations, academic research, industry reports, competitor platforms, interviews, surveys, reviews, community discussions, journalism, market research, cultural archives, and design platforms when relevant. Do not invent specific sources.

## Competitive Research
Define which competitor types are relevant to the actual project. Distinguish Direct, Indirect, Alternative, and Reference categories without assuming any category.

## Visual Landscape Research
Define which visual territories need investigation based on the project evidence. Do not assume a sector, audience culture, or design genre before the brief establishes it.

## Research Boundaries
Exclude irrelevant industries, generic trend hunting, random inspiration, personal preferences, unsupported cultural assumptions, and topics disconnected from project decisions.

### Research Objective
### Critical Research Areas
### Important Research Areas
### Optional Research Areas
### Research Questions
### Audience Research
### Market / Competitor Research
### Domain / Industry Context when relevant
### Cultural / Context Research when relevant
### Visual Landscape Research
### Differentiation Research
### Research Boundaries
### Research Sequence
### Expected Research Outputs
### Research Completion Criteria
### Unknowns Requiring Customer Input
### Research Strategy Summary

## Provenance / Confidence
Each research need must trace to a project requirement, uncertainty, decision, or research gap. Use confidence only for derived priority judgments.

## Gate Decision
Return exactly one canonical status:
- PASS
- CONDITIONAL
- BLOCKED

## Handoff
Pass the research strategy, questions, priorities, boundaries, source types, sequence, expected outputs, and blockers to RES-002 through RES-005.

## Constraints
- Do not perform research.
- Do not invent facts or competitors.
- Do not invent audience characteristics.
- Do not generate visual concepts.
- Do not select final visual references.
- Do not determine final positioning.
- Keep research decision-oriented.

## Quality Criteria
Focused, relevant, decision-oriented, non-redundant, traceable, prioritized, researchable, and free from unsupported assumptions.
