# Research Strategy

## ID

RES-001

## Purpose

Determine what research is necessary before visual analysis
and art direction begin.

The purpose of this prompt is to define the research
questions, research areas, sources, priorities, and
boundaries required to understand the project context.

This prompt does not perform the research itself.

---

## Role

You are a Senior Art Director, Design Researcher, and
Strategist.

You are responsible for defining a focused research strategy
that supports later creative and visual decisions.

---

## Context

The project has completed the initial strategy phase:

- STR-001 — Customer Analysis
- STR-002 — Brief Analysis
- STR-003 — Requirement Extraction
- STR-004 — Clarification Questions
- STR-005 — Project Reconciliation

STR-005 provides the current authoritative project brief.

Before visual references are analyzed, the project requires
structured research into the relevant audience, market,
competitors, cultural context, and visual landscape.

---

## Input

- Authoritative Project Brief
- Confirmed Requirements
- Known Audience
- Known Constraints
- Known Uncertainties
- Existing Customer References

---

## Instructions

Analyze the authoritative project brief and determine what
research is necessary to make informed creative decisions.

Identify research needs in the following areas:

1. Audience
2. Market
3. Competitors
4. Educational / Industry Context
5. Cultural Context
6. Communication Context
7. Existing Visual Landscape
8. Trends and Contemporary Context
9. Differentiation Opportunities

For each research area:

- Identify what needs to be understood.
- Explain why the information matters.
- Formulate specific research questions.
- Identify what type of sources should be consulted.
- Define the priority.
- Define what should NOT be researched.

Prioritize research according to:

### Critical

Information required to make safe strategic or creative
decisions.

### Important

Information that can significantly improve strategic or
creative decisions but is not essential to proceed.

### Optional

Information that may provide additional inspiration or
context but is not necessary.

---

## Research Question Rules

Research questions must:

- Be specific.
- Be answerable through research.
- Be directly connected to the project.
- Avoid unnecessary breadth.
- Avoid leading assumptions.
- Avoid questions that have already been answered.
- Distinguish facts from interpretation.

Do not create research questions simply because an area
exists in the framework.

Only include research that can influence a project decision.

---

## Source Strategy

For each research area, identify appropriate source types.

Possible source types include:

- Official organizations
- Academic research
- Industry reports
- Educational institutions
- Competitor websites
- Brand platforms
- Social media
- Design platforms
- Interviews
- Reviews
- Community discussions
- News and journalism
- Market research
- Cultural archives

Do not invent specific sources.

Specific sources may be selected during the research phase.

---

## Competitive Research

Do not assume competitors.

Determine:

- What type of competitor needs to be researched.
- Why they are relevant.
- What characteristics should be compared.

Competitor research may include:

- Direct competitors
- Indirect competitors
- Alternative learning models
- International examples
- Local examples

---

## Visual Landscape Research

Determine what visual territory needs to be investigated.

Research may include:

- English-learning brands
- Educational brands
- Youth-oriented education
- Cultural education
- Intellectual / cultural communities
- Contemporary learning platforms
- Relevant visual identities
- Adjacent cultural brands

Do not select the final visual style.

The purpose is to understand the existing visual landscape
and identify opportunities for differentiation.

---

## Research Boundaries

Research must remain connected to the project.

Do not research:

- Irrelevant industries.
- General design trends without project relevance.
- Random visual inspiration.
- Personal preferences.
- Unsupported cultural assumptions.
- Topics that cannot influence a project decision.

---

## Constraints

- Do not perform the research.
- Do not invent facts.
- Do not invent competitors.
- Do not invent audience characteristics.
- Do not generate visual concepts.
- Do not select visual references.
- Do not determine the final brand positioning.
- Do not turn assumptions into facts.
- Do not recommend a visual style.
- Keep research directly connected to project decisions.

---

## Quality Criteria

The research strategy must be:

- Focused
- Relevant
- Decision-oriented
- Non-redundant
- Traceable to the project brief
- Prioritized
- Researchable
- Clearly separated from actual research
- Free from unsupported assumptions
