# Motif & Sign Extraction Output — Paul Klee / English Beyond Language

## Purpose

This record demonstrates the motif-extraction layer on the supplied English Beyond Language visual references described as Paul Klee–inspired.

It records recurring visual building blocks before they are translated into an Instagram template.

## Source Scope

- Supplied EBL visual identity boards / template references.
- Named style: Paul Klee.
- Application target: Instagram.
- This is a project-specific extraction record, not a claim that every element is universally characteristic of Klee.

## Extraction Principles

Extract the visual vocabulary as observed in the references while preserving the distinction between:

1. observed motif;
2. formal behavior;
3. project interpretation;
4. later EBL adaptation.

Do not copy a specific artwork or reproduce a specific historical composition.

## 01. Eyes / Eye Signs

**Observed form**
- Simplified almond / eye contour.
- Circular or oval pupil / inner mark.
- Often drawn with a single black contour.
- Can appear as a standalone symbol or embedded in a composition.

**Formal behavior**
- Minimal line construction.
- Strong black-on-paper contrast.
- Slight hand-drawn irregularity.
- Large enough to act as a focal sign, or small enough to act as a recurring motif.

**Role**
- Attention.
- Observation.
- Looking / seeing.

**EBL transfer candidate**
- Suitable as a restrained symbol for curiosity, observation, or perspective.

---

## 02. Spiral / Coiled Line

**Observed form**
- Simple inward spiral.
- Single continuous gesture.
- Often irregular rather than mathematically perfect.

**Formal behavior**
- Line-led rather than filled.
- Organic movement.
- Creates visual rhythm and inward motion.

**Role**
- Curiosity.
- Movement of thought.
- Exploration.

**EBL transfer candidate**
- Useful for concepts involving thought, discovery, language development, or reflection.

## 03. Hand-Drawn Human / Face Profile

# Extracted Formal Rules

1. Prefer simplified symbolic forms over realistic illustration.
2. Allow linework to remain visibly human and slightly irregular.
3. Combine organic forms with geometric fields.
4. Use color fields as compositional masses, not decoration alone.
5. Use small signs as visual punctuation.
6. Balance large color masses with small dark marks or symbols.
7. Preserve generous negative space.
8. Use partial grids to introduce structure without losing painterly freedom.
9. Avoid perfect symmetry and mechanical repetition.
10. Let line, shape, and color interact rather than occupy isolated compartments.

# Instagram Translation Constraints

This extraction is not yet the Instagram template.

For Instagram, later stages must select motifs according to:

- readability at mobile scale
- semantic relevance to the content
- repeatability
- brand fit
- visual hierarchy
- slide continuity
- accessibility

Not every extracted motif should appear in every post.

# Non-Transfer Rule

Do not reproduce a specific Klee painting, exact composition, exact symbol arrangement, or distinctive artwork.

The extraction records a visual vocabulary and its formal behavior for controlled transformation.
