# English Beyond Language — Instagram Story Template Rules

## Status

Current state: `PROJECT_REFERENCE / REVIEW_CANDIDATE`

This file records the Story-template system learned and produced for English Beyond Language. Exact coordinates/composition are not promoted to an approved reusable master until explicit human approval.

## Platform

```text
Instagram Story
1080 × 1920 px
9:16
```

## Core Story Principle

An EBL Story must inherit the English Beyond Language visual identity **without simply stretching a feed post vertically**.

The Story template should behave as a live communication surface with room for:

- hook/question;
- short teaching point;
- cultural observation;
- quote;
- poll/question sticker;
- link/CTA;
- short sequence continuation.

## Visual Structure

Preferred Story grammar:

- warm ivory/paper ground;
- large central negative space;
- painterly visual weight near edges/corners;
- restrained rust/mustard/navy/green/charcoal palette;
- small line/dot/sign motifs;
- asymmetry with clear focal hierarchy;
- fixed secondary brand signature;
- no generic infographic frame.

## Content Zones

A Story template should define flexible zones rather than hard decorative boxes:

```text
TOP_SAFE / TITLE ENTRY
MAIN_MESSAGE_ZONE
SECONDARY_COPY_ZONE
INTERACTIVE_STICKER_ZONE
BRAND_SIGNATURE_ZONE
BOTTOM_SAFE / CTA SUPPORT
```

Exact pixel values may vary by approved template version, but essential information must stay clear of platform UI obstruction.

## Typography

Use EBL semantic typography:

```text
CONTENT MEANING
→ STORY FUNCTION
→ READING ORDER
→ FONT ROLE
→ ALIGNMENT
→ SPACING
→ EMPHASIS
```

General tendency:

- editorial serif for conceptual/question/cultural/reflective content;
- sans serif for direct/practical/explanatory content.

Do not use font changes as decoration alone.

## Logo

Use the chosen **Geometric Reader Integrated Logo** or an approved production-signature variant.

Rules:

- logo remains secondary;
- keep clear space;
- do not regenerate the logo;
- do not add decorative support objects;
- do not place essential content into the logo zone;
- repeated Story sets should preserve stable logo role and anchor logic.

## Interactive Story Rule

Reusable clean Story masters should preserve room for Instagram-native interactive elements.

Do not bake fake Instagram UI into the clean template unless the human explicitly requests a presentation mockup.

## Current Candidate Direction

The current generated candidate established:

- vertical warm paper composition;
- navy painterly weight upper-left;
- mustard/rust painterly weight upper-right;
- large clean central reading field;
- small dot/sign language;
- lower visual counterweight;
- chosen logo signature near lower-left;
- lower CTA/support line.

This is a **directional candidate**, not yet an approved reusable Story master.

## Approval Gate

Before promotion to `APPROVED_STORY_MASTER`, human review must confirm:

- visual identity;
- logo application;
- central content flexibility;
- safe-zone behavior;
- typography system;
- interactive-sticker usability;
- repeatability across different story types;
- whether exact composition/coordinates should become locked.
