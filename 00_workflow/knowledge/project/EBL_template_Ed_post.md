## Typography System

Typography follows meaning.

### Hierarchy

1. **Primary conceptual title** — dominant visual weight.
2. **Key semantic contrast / phrase** — accent color, weight or placement where meaning requires it.
3. **Supporting explanation** — readable and subordinate to the title.
4. **Examples / list content** — may use controlled looseness and asymmetry while remaining readable.
5. **CTA / final action** — stronger weight and clear ending signal.

### Semantic color

Accent color is not decorative. Rust/red marks conceptual emphasis, contrast or action.

### Micro-adjustment rule

Prefer small changes such as:

- slightly bigger / smaller;
- selective bold;
- word- or phrase-level color;
- line-break refinement;
- controlled left/right movement;
- spacing and rhythm adjustment.

Do not introduce large typographic style changes unless explicitly requested.

## Controlled Looseness

Lists or multiple supporting statements do not have to form a rigid grid.

Approved behavior includes:

- alternating left/right placement;
- staggered vertical rhythm;
- unequal but intentional spacing;
- controlled visual scattering;
- clear reading order despite asymmetry.

The goal is **alive, editorial rhythm**, not random disorder.

## Approved Slide-by-Slide Template Logic

### Slide 1 — Question / Cover

Function: introduce the central concept.

Approved structure:

- `01 / 06` at top-left;
- large ochre painterly field;
- main question: `WHAT IS ENGLISH INDEPENDENCE?`;
- supporting statement below;
- phrase-level rust emphasis on `things that matter`;
- blue architectural/arch form at lower-right;
- hand-drawn upper line, rust circle, dot-grid;
- EBL production signature bottom-left.

### Slide 2 — Blockage

Function: describe the gap between knowledge and real-time use.

Approved structure:

- direct sans-serif opening statements;
- `But still...` in rust/red;
- stronger bold problem statement;
- painterly fields at corners;
- hand-drawn line, black dot, grid fragment;
- EBL production signature bottom-left.

### Slide 3 — Obstacles / Controlled Scatter

Function: show six situations in which English can still get in the way.

Approved title:

- `Sometimes English` — black;
- `still gets in the way` — rust/red;
- `of what matters to you...` — black.

Approved supporting statements preserve this order:

1. Taking an academic or professional opportunity.
2. Writing something important.
3. Having a real conversation.
4. Following a lecture.
5. Using AI effectively.
6. Finishing a book.

Approved placement principle:

- alternating / scattered left-right rhythm;
- no rigid two-column table appearance;
- readable, controlled and editorial;
- existing painterly geometry, curved line, dots, plant sign and logo remain fixed.

### Slide 4 — Perspective Shift

Function: contrast knowledge quantity with practical agency.

Approved structure:

- `So maybe the real question is not:`;
- ochre painted strip containing `How much English do I know?`;
- `but:`;
- deep navy painted strip containing `What can I actually do through English?`;
- large curved line and painterly corner elements;
- EBL production signature bottom-left.

### Slide 5 — Agency Definition

Function: define English Independence.

Approved semantic emphasis:

- `English Independence` as dominant title;
- `means being able to use` in black;
- **`the English you have.` in rust/red**;
- two supporting statements only:
  1. `To find your way when you don’t know everything.`
  2. `To understand, respond, learn, and create meaning for yourself.`
- blue and rust circular markers;
- blue spiral field lower-right;
- EBL production signature bottom-left.

### Slide 6 — Expansion / CTA

Function: reject perfection as the goal and end on participation/agency.

Approved title hierarchy:

- first line: **`The goal is not`** — black, one line;
- second line: **`perfect`** — rust/red;
- second line: **`English.`** — black;
- `perfect English.` remains one emphasized line.

Approved supporting hierarchy:

- `It is to stop English from standing between you and:` — supporting explanation;
- navy concept field containing `ideas • culture • people • knowledge / study • work • opportunities`;
- `English should help you enter a wider world — and take part in it.` — explanatory conclusion;
- **`Become independent in English.`** — rust/red and bold CTA;
- all non-typographic artwork remains unchanged.

## Failure Conditions

Fail the output if any of these occur without explicit human approval:

- redesigning an approved slide while performing a text edit;
- changing unrelated painterly shapes, logo, linework or texture;
- altering approved text meaning;
- losing slide numbering format;
- replacing the EBL logo with a regenerated approximation;
- turning controlled looseness into random disorder;
- applying accent color decoratively rather than semantically;
- changing a word-level emphasis beyond the authorized phrase;
- reconstructing the template from memory when the canonical visual artifact is available.
