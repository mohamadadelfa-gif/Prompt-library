# English Beyond Language — “A Line for Today” Story Template

## Status

Project reference / current working template. This may evolve through human-approved iterations.

## Purpose

Create a recognizable Instagram Story series for **English Beyond Language** where the quote is the primary focus and the visual context supports the meaning of the quote.

This file is project knowledge, not an executable Art Direction prompt.

## Core Layout

### 1. Series Header
- Text: **A LINE FOR TODAY**
- Position: top center.
- Use only once in the composition.
- Keep it secondary to the quote.

### 2. Brand Logo
- Position: top right.
- Use the approved English Beyond Language logo/series mark.
- Do not duplicate “A Line for Today” inside or behind the logo if the series header already appears.
- Branding remains secondary to the quote.

### 3. Quote Area
- Position: upper-middle / central area.
- This is the dominant visual hierarchy.
- Allocate generous negative space around the quote.
- Typography may respond to literary/historical/cultural context, but readability remains primary.
- Exact quote wording is locked when the human supplies or approves an exact quotation.

### 4. Author + Source
- Position: directly below the quote.
- Author should be clearly visible.
- Source/work title may be smaller but must remain readable.
- Attribution must be verified or explicitly marked unresolved before publication.

### 5. Contextual Image
- Role: supporting context, not the primary focus.
- Prefer imagery that reinforces the meaning/action of the quote.
- The image may occupy lower/background areas while preserving quote readability.
- Important contextual action should remain visually clear.

### 6. EBL Graphic Layer
- Use a thin, restrained geometric layer derived from the approved EBL visual system.
- Typical elements may include circles, arches, rectangular fields, fine lines, dots, grids, and cropped geometric forms.
- Keep these mostly near edges/corners so they frame rather than compete.
- Use approved EBL palette behavior rather than introducing random decorative colors.

## Visual Hierarchy

1. Quote — primary focus
2. Author / source — attribution
3. Contextual image/action — meaning support
4. A LINE FOR TODAY — series identifier
5. EBL logo — brand identifier
6. Geometric decoration — supporting layer

## Composition Logic

```text
Top center              A LINE FOR TODAY
Top right               EBL logo
Upper-middle / center   Quote
Below quote             Author + source
Lower/background        Contextual image/action
Edges/corners           Restrained EBL geometric layer
```

## Writing → Design Rule

The Writing workflow supplies:

- exact quote wording;
- verified or unresolved attribution state;
- author/work/date information;
- any supporting contextual copy;
- language-level constraints;
- locked/flexible wording state.

Design may format the approved quote and attribution but may not paraphrase or change their meaning.

Use:

`00_workflow/workflows/cross_workflow_handoff_contract.md`

## Remove / Avoid

- no extra footer branding unless explicitly requested;
- no unrelated side keyword lists;
- no repeated “A Line for Today” label;
- no decorative shapes without a communication role;
- no free redesign when only a local edit is requested;
- no paraphrasing an approved exact quote;
- no invented attribution or context.

## QC

Before approval, check:

- exact quote spelling;
- author spelling;
- source/work/date accuracy or unresolved status;
- quote readability at Story size;
- author/source readability;
- text/background contrast;
- logo fidelity and placement;
- “A LINE FOR TODAY” appears only once;
- no duplicate branding;
- contextual image supports the quote;
- geometric layer remains restrained;
- approved composition is preserved when only a local edit is requested;
- exact approved Writing version is traceable.

## Current Design Principle

The template stays recognizable while the photograph, contextual typography, and content-specific details may change according to the approved quote and meaning.

## Provenance

Derived from the human-established English Beyond Language “A Line for Today” Story production pattern and subsequent template structuralization. Exact quote content remains content-specific and must preserve its own source/attribution provenance.
