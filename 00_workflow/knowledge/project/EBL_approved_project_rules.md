## EBL-RULE-002 — Core Communication Direction

English Beyond Language frames English as a means of independent participation, not merely grammar/vocabulary knowledge.

Current project concept:

```text
Tell → Explain → Argue → Participate
```

The Instagram system should ultimately help the right B2+ learner:

```text
recognize a relevant problem
→ build trust
→ understand the method
→ see practical/cultural value
→ move toward consultation / class inquiry / registration
```

Status: `PROJECT_DECISION`

## EBL-RULE-003 — Visual Character

The approved visual direction is contemporary editorial/cultural with restrained painterly character.

Current direction:

- communication clarity remains primary;
- painterly/material character supports meaning rather than replacing it;
- warm ivory/paper grounds;
- rust/burnt orange;
- ochre;
- deep desaturated blue/navy or deep green;
- muted olive;
- charcoal/black;
- display serif for major questions/titles;
- clean support typography for secondary information;
- asymmetry and negative space;
- imperfect geometric/natural forms;
- hand-drawn lines;
- irregular rectangles/color fields;
- circles, arches, grids, dots, organic forms, signs and gestures;
- restrained tactile paper/material texture.

Desired mood:

- curious
- human
- thoughtful
- warm
- playful but intelligent
- exploratory
- culturally aware
- editorial

Status: `APPROVED_RULE`

---

## EBL-RULE-004 — No Infographic-Style Default

Do not produce infographic-style visuals by default for EBL.

Avoid as the primary visual grammar:

- icon rows
- boxed information systems
- diagram-like layouts
- feature grids
- dashboards
- excessive labels
- generic explainer-infographic composition

Prefer:

- editorial visual essays
- one strong visual idea per slide
- painterly/geometric composition
- meaningful signs and forms
- generous negative space
- restrained typography
- narrative progression across slides

Infographic-like structures may be used only when explicitly requested or when necessary for a specific information task and approved by the human.

Status: `HUMAN_PREFERENCE` + `APPROVED_RULE`

---

## EBL-RULE-005 — Klee-Informed Use

Paul Klee research may inform EBL through principles, not literal artwork copying.

Relevant transferable principles include:

- visible ↔ legible relationships;
- sequential, reading-like looking;
- signs between resemblance and convention;
- graphic abstraction;
- line as movement/path;
- map/chronicle/page-like spatial organization;
- incomplete forms that invite imaginative continuation;
- meaningful symbolic micro-details;
- drawing/painting synthesis.

Do not reproduce a specific Klee artwork or symbol literally.

Use the source package in:

`00_workflow/knowledge/external/paul_klee_sources.md`

Status: `SOURCE_DERIVED` → `APPROVED_RULE`

## EBL-RULE-007 — Logo Use on Carousel Slides

For approved carousel artwork, branding should remain visually secondary to the content unless the human explicitly requests a different treatment.

When revising an already approved slide:

- change only the requested branding area;
- do not redesign unrelated slide content to accommodate the logo;
- preserve approved artwork, typography, texture, colors, numbering, and composition.

The newly chosen locked primary logo does **not** automatically replace logos inside already approved Post 01 files. Retroactive replacement requires an explicit human instruction because the chosen master includes its own black presentation background/glow and may require a different placement decision.

Status: `HUMAN_PREFERENCE` + `APPROVED_RULE`

For approved EBL artwork, preserve everything outside the explicitly requested change scope.

## EBL-RULE-009 — Teacher Portrait / Profile Image

The supplied teacher portrait is the current teacher identity reference for EBL profile-picture work.

Profile-picture treatment must:

- preserve recognizable facial features;
- prioritize face recognition over decorative branding;
- remain natural rather than beauty-retouched or AI-reconstructed;
- use a quiet background and sufficient separation;
- be tested as a circular avatar and at small sizes;
- follow `QC-IG-PROFILE-001`.

Do not automatically apply the full EBL post-design system to the profile picture unless a separate branded-avatar variant is explicitly requested.
