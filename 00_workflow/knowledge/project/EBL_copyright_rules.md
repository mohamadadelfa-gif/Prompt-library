# EBL Copyright Knowledge

## Status

Current working copyright guidance for English Beyond Language (EBL) film-based Instagram content.

This document is a practical production rule set, not legal advice. Copyright exceptions such as fair use are case-by-case and depend on jurisdiction and context.

## 1. Film Clip

- Use only the shortest portion necessary for teaching, analysis, criticism, or cultural discussion.
- There is no guaranteed safe 10-, 20-, or 30-second rule.
- Prefer a transformative structure: **short clip → pause/freeze → EBL text → recorded explanation → optional short return to clip**.
- Do not use copyrighted film footage merely as decorative background unless EBL has the necessary rights or license.
- Credit the film/source when relevant, but remember: **credit is not permission**.

## 2. Original Film Audio / Soundtrack

- Keep original film dialogue or soundtrack only when it directly supports the lesson, analysis, or commentary.
- Do not assume that extracting a short soundtrack segment makes it copyright-safe.
- If the exact audio is available through Instagram's licensed Music/Audio library for the intended post type and account, prefer adding it through Instagram rather than embedding a ripped soundtrack into the exported edit.

## 3. Background Music

Preferred sources, in order of practical safety:

1. Original music owned by EBL or properly cleared for EBL use.
2. Properly licensed royalty-free/commercial music.
3. Meta Sound Collection where its license fits the intended use.
4. Instagram's licensed Music/Audio library for eligible organic posts.

For boosted posts or advertising, prefer original or commercially cleared royalty-free music rather than ordinary Instagram licensed music.

## 4. Fair-Use / Copyright-Exception Context

A stronger fair-use or copyright-exception context generally has these characteristics:

- **Purpose:** teaching, criticism, commentary, analysis, research, parody, or news rather than simple entertainment.
- **Transformation:** add substantial new meaning through pause/freeze, EBL text, explanation, interpretation, or voice-over.
- **Amount:** use only the minimum portion necessary for the specific teaching or commentary purpose.
- **Importance:** avoid taking more of the central or most significant part of the original work than the explanation requires.
- **Market effect:** the EBL post should not function as a substitute for watching, buying, streaming, or licensing the original work.
- **Case-by-case:** educational use strengthens the context but does not automatically make a use lawful.

## EBL Core Rule

> **Use only what you need → transform it → teach/comment on it → don't replace the original.**

## EBL Preferred Film-Post Structure

**Short film clip + relevant dialogue/audio → pause/freeze → EBL teaching text → EBL recorded explanation → licensed/original/royalty-free background music.**
