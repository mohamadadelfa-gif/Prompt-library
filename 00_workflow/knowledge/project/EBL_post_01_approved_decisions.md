## Narrative Function

Post 01 is a six-slide visual essay.

The approved narrative progression is:

```text
Slide 1 → QUESTION
Slide 2 → BLOCKAGE
Slide 3 → OBSTACLES
Slide 4 → SHIFT IN PERSPECTIVE
Slide 5 → AGENCY
Slide 6 → EXPANSION
```

The sequence should feel like a visual argument, not a six-panel information sheet.

Status: `PROJECT_DECISION` + `APPROVED_RULE` for Post 01.

---

## Slide 1 — Question

Role: open the conceptual problem and make the learner question the difference between knowing English and using English independently.

Approved copy:

**WHAT IS ENGLISH INDEPENDENCE?**

`You may know a lot of English.`

`But can you use it to do the things that matter to you?`

Approved emphasis — latest human correction:

- only `things that matter` is rust/red;
- `the` remains black;
- `to you?` remains black;
- the slide establishes the master editorial tone for the carousel.

Do not recolor the whole final phrase.

---

## Slide 2 — Blockage

Role: make the learner recognize the gap between knowledge and real-time use.

Approved meaning:

- studied English;
- knows grammar;
- knows words;
- the idea exists internally;
- English does not follow when needed.

Do not turn this slide into a generic grammar lesson.

---

## Slide 3 — Obstacles

Role: show where English can still obstruct things that matter to the learner.

Approved content categories:

- academic/professional opportunity;
- real conversation;
- using AI effectively;
- writing something important;
- following a lecture;
- finishing a book.

Approved layout principle:

- two-column/support structure may be used;
- avoid generic bullet/icon infographic treatment when the editorial composition can communicate the content more naturally.

---

## Slide 4 — Shift in Perspective

Role: reframe the learner's question from quantity of knowledge to practical agency.

Approved logic:

`How much English do I know?`

becomes:

`What can I actually do through English?`

Approved emphasis decision:

- `real question` should remain black rather than being turned into decorative color emphasis.

---

## Slide 5 — Agency

Role: define English Independence as the ability to use available English autonomously.

Approved core statement:

**English Independence**

`means being able to use the English you have.`

Approved supporting points — exactly two:

1. `To find your way when you don’t know everything.`
2. `To understand, respond, learn, and create meaning for yourself.`

Critical content rule:

**Do not add a third supporting point.**

The previously generated third line/point is unauthorized and must remain removed.

Typography integrity correction:

- black/charcoal text must remain visually solid and intentional;
- do not leave pale halos, faded edges, ghosted duplicate edges, washed black, or inpainting residue around Slide 5 text;
- preserve the approved font, size, line breaks, alignment and letterform proportions while restoring raster clarity;
- validate Slide 5 typography at native resolution under `QC-TYPE-001`.

Slide-number correction:

- Slide 5 must use the same carousel numbering system as the other slides;
- approved format: `05 / 06`;
- match the same top-left anchor, font logic, weight relationship, size relationship, spacing around the slash, and color used by Slides 1–4 and 6;
- `05` alone is not acceptable.

Status: explicit human correction, approved 2026-08-15.

---

## Slide 6 — Expansion

Role: conclude the visual essay by opening from individual agency into a wider world of participation, knowledge, culture, work and opportunity.

Approved semantic hierarchy:

1. concluding thesis;
2. what English should no longer stand between the learner and;
3. expansion field / concepts;
4. wider-world bridge statement;
5. final CTA.

Approved title logic:

`The goal is not`

**`perfect English.`**

Typography refinement:

- title should use the same editorial serif logic established by the carousel;
- `The goal is not` is primary black serif;
- `perfect English.` is the emphasized rust serif line;
- left-align the title with the established text column;
- do not redesign the surrounding painterly composition.

Approved supporting meaning:

`It is to stop English from standing between you and:`

Concept field:

- ideas
- culture
- people
- knowledge
- study
- work
- opportunities

Bridge:

`English should help you enter a wider world — and take part in it.`

CTA:

`Become independent in English.`
