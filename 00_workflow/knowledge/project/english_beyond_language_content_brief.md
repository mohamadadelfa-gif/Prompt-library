## Brand Foundation

### SOURCE_FACT

- Account name: **Keyvan | English Beyond Language**.
- Strategic concept: **English Independence**.
- Current bio:
  - Ideas • Culture • Communication
  - Speak clearly. Express complex ideas.
  - Become independent in English.
  - Free 20-minute English consultation.
- The intended audience is serious B2+ learners, including professionals, academics, and internationally oriented adults.

### SUPPLIED_DIRECTION

English Beyond Language should help learners use English independently rather than collect grammar rules or isolated vocabulary. Independence means being able to communicate, think, understand, respond, read, and participate without constant dependence on preparation, translation, teachers, or simplified learner material.

## Public Learning Framework

### SUPPLIED_DIRECTION

The public framework is:

1. **TELL** — narrate experiences, events, memories, and stories naturally.
2. **EXPLAIN** — make ideas understandable through definitions, examples, reformulation, comparison, and movement between abstract and concrete ideas.
3. **ARGUE** — form and defend an opinion using claims, reasons, examples, qualification, counterargument, and response.
4. **PARTICIPATE** — enter existing cultural, intellectual, professional, academic, and public conversations conducted in English.

These are not four separate courses. They are four observable expressions of English independence. Grammar, vocabulary, reading, writing, listening, and pronunciation support all four.

## Launch Content Sequence

### SUPPLIED_DIRECTION

The first six posts establish the philosophy before Keyvan appears directly:

1. **What Is English Independence?** — distinguish knowing English from using it independently.
2. **How Do You Build English Independence?** — introduce Tell, Explain, Argue, Participate as the account map.
3. **TELL** — use a strong real storytelling example, analyze it, teach useful English, and issue a small challenge.
4. **EXPLAIN** — analyze a strong real explanation and extract techniques and useful English.
5. **ARGUE** — analyze a genuine argument or disagreement through claim, reason, example, qualification, counterargument, and response.
6. **PARTICIPATE** — show English as access to ideas, culture, knowledge, people, and opportunities beyond the learner's first-language environment.

Post 7 introduces Keyvan through a short personal video focused on the problem of English being available when needed. Teaching experience supports authority but should not become the headline.

## Sustainable Publishing System

### SUPPLIED_DIRECTION

- Initial rhythm: three feed posts per week.
- Approximate monthly balance:
  - 70% educational;
  - 20% cultural/personal;
  - 10% promotional.
- Percentages are monthly guidance, not a rigid weekly quota.

Recommended weekly roles:

1. **Keyvan-led teaching post** — generally video-based and structured as Question → Example → Explanation → Useful English → Challenge.
2. **Educational analysis or reflection** — carousel, text/image, annotated example, comparison, clip/slides, or question/breakdown.
3. **Culture or Participation post** — Source → Idea → English → Question.

The content system should remain sustainable rather than becoming a complicated daily publishing machine.

## Content and Teaching Principles

### SUPPLIED_DIRECTION

- Questions should often appear before logos or brand introductions and may become a recurring identity device.
- Grammar should be connected to communicative meaning rather than taught as isolated textbook content.
- Reading strategy is central to English Independence.
- Error correction should explain intent and cause while building confidence rather than shame.
- Culture should generate communication, not decorate the account.
- Personal content should reveal Keyvan's teaching mind and cultural attention without requiring private-life exposure.
- Feed posts form the educational archive; Stories support lighter interaction and personality.
- Lives should begin infrequently, approximately monthly.
- Each post after the launch sequence should work independently; the account must not behave like a curriculum that requires sequential consumption.

Recurring subject areas may include reading, grammar, contextual vocabulary, authentic errors, writing, listening, and independent-learning practices when they support Tell, Explain, Argue, Participate, or reduced dependence.

## Human Presence and Media

### SUPPLIED_DIRECTION

- A small talking-head-over-content format may become a signature teaching format approximately once per week, but it is not compulsory for every post.
- Keyvan's face and voice provide trust, continuity, personality, authority, and human connection.
- Slides and source material provide clarity, structure, visual interest, and examples.
- Use Keyvan's real voice for Keyvan-led posts.
- Use source audio only where legally and practically appropriate.
- Do not add audio or voiceover merely to satisfy a format expectation.

## Promotion

### SOURCE_FACT

- Primary CTA: **Free 20-minute English consultation**.
- The consultation is a short conversation about level, problems, goals, and program suitability; it is not a free complete lesson or detailed assessment.

### SUPPLIED_DIRECTION

Promotion may cover consultation, class openings, testimonials, anonymized progress, learner before/after language, IELTS services, intensives, or workshops.

Avoid fake urgency, excessive discounts, exaggerated fluency promises, constant enrollment messaging, and learner shaming. The stated principle is **strong persuasion without manipulation**.

## Visual and Creative Knowledge

### HUMAN_PREFERENCE

- Desired character: cultural, intelligent, human, curious, and serious enough for advanced learners.
- Target balance: approximately **70% editorial clarity / 30% artistic texture**.
- Paul Klee should remain an influence rather than an obvious theme.
- Headlines, questions, examples, subtitles, and video frames require strong visual dominance and clarity.
- Hand-painted blocks, imperfect lines, small symbols, and warm textures may provide secondary personality.
- Reduce the symbol vocabulary; too many unrelated symbols create competing visual voices.
- The question mark may become a distinctive recurring motif.

### SUPPLIED TYPOGRAPHY DIRECTION

- **Inter** — primary working typeface for educational content.
- **Cormorant Garamond** — selective accent for cultural/editorial posts, quotations, literature, or occasional headlines.
- **Vazirmatn** — Persian-language typeface candidate.

### SUPPLIED CONTENT-MIX DIRECTION

The feed may alternate naturally among:

- Klee-influenced educational design;
- cultural photography;
- film or music material;
- Keyvan's face and video;
- occasional text-only editorial posts.

The visual system should not repeat one template indefinitely.

## Core Creative Principle

### SUPPLIED_DIRECTION

**Structured underneath. Alive on the surface.**

The underlying system is rigorous and educational. The visible experience should feel like entering an intelligent, culturally curious English classroom rather than following a conventional language institute.
