# QC-AUD-001 — Audience Catcher QC

## Purpose
Validate whether an Instagram candidate can earn and sustain the intended audience's attention without sacrificing EBL's positioning, meaning or visual identity. This is not a clickbait optimizer. It tests relevance, stopping power, curiosity, comprehension and continuation intent for the defined audience.

## Input
- EBL audience knowledge / target segment
- Approved content objective
- Candidate carousel/reel/cover
- Locked copy
- Narrative role of each slide
- EBL brand/voice rules

## Output
- PASS / PASS_WITH_REVISION / FAIL
- Audience-catcher findings
- Hook/retention risks
- Revision instructions
- Human approval requirement

## Gates

### AUD-01 Audience Relevance — mandatory
Can the intended B2+ learner recognize a meaningful problem, aspiration, tension or opportunity relevant to them? Reject generic language-school hooks that could belong to any beginner-learning account.

### AUD-02 First-Frame Stopping Power
At feed size, does the first slide/cover create a clear reason to stop within roughly 1–2 seconds? Check headline clarity, visual focal point, contrast, specificity and tension. The hook must remain truthful to the approved content.

### AUD-03 Curiosity Gap Without Clickbait
Does the opening create an unanswered but legitimate question that the carousel can resolve? Fail manipulative, exaggerated or unsupported promises.

### AUD-04 Immediate Comprehension
Can the audience quickly understand what territory the post concerns? Mystery is allowed only when it increases curiosity without making the subject unclear.

### AUD-05 Personal Identification
Does the post allow the target learner to recognize their own experience, e.g. knowing grammar/words but being unable to act through English? Prefer concrete lived situations over abstract educational claims.

### AUD-06 Value Promise
Is there a credible reason to continue? The audience should anticipate insight, reframing, practical understanding or cultural/intellectual value—not merely decoration.

### AUD-07 Slide-to-Slide Retention
Each slide should create a reason to advance to the next. Check information pacing, open loops, escalation, variation and narrative momentum. Avoid revealing the entire conclusion on slide 1.

### AUD-08 Cognitive Load
Check whether text volume, visual noise, jargon or excessive symbolism creates abandonment risk. The design may be intellectually serious while remaining scannable.

### AUD-09 Emotional Tone Fit
The attention mechanism must fit EBL: intelligent, calm, curious, independent, culturally aware and respectful. Avoid fear, shame, infantilization, urgency tricks and generic motivational language.

### AUD-10 Distinctiveness
Would the post be recognizable as more than generic English-learning content? Check conceptual framing, voice and visual identity. Distinctiveness must come from EBL's proposition and visual grammar, not novelty for novelty's sake.

### AUD-11 Payoff
Does the final slide satisfy or advance the question/tension established at the beginning? The audience should receive a meaningful conceptual payoff and appropriate next orientation/CTA.

### AUD-12 Human Audience Judgment
Model evaluation is provisional. Human review decides whether the hook feels credible, attractive and appropriate for the actual audience.

## Suggested scoring
Mandatory: AUD-01 must pass.
- First-frame stopping power: 15
- Curiosity: 10
- Immediate comprehension: 10
- Personal identification: 15
- Value promise: 10
- Retention: 15
- Cognitive load: 10
- Tone fit: 5
- Distinctiveness: 5
- Payoff: 5
Total: 100

A critical truth/brand/audience mismatch => FAIL regardless of score.

## Evidence record
Record: candidate ID, audience segment, gate, observation, severity, evidence in the candidate, revision action, and learning scope (post-specific vs reusable EBL rule).

## Relationship to QC-IG-001
QC-IG-001 asks: "Does this work correctly as an EBL Instagram visual?"
QC-AUD-001 asks: "Will the intended audience stop, understand, identify, continue and receive a payoff?"
Both must pass before final human approval and Figma handoff.
