# Decision Gates

Decision gates prevent the workflow from advancing on incomplete or contradictory work.

## Strategy

### PASS
Required strategy outputs are complete, traceable, and internally consistent.

### CONDITIONAL
The work can continue only with explicitly listed non-blocking conditions.

### BLOCKED
A critical unknown, contradiction, or missing approval prevents reliable downstream work.

## Research

PASS only when the required research questions have sufficient evidence and unresolved gaps are documented.

BLOCK when a required conclusion would depend on unsupported inference.

## Visual Analysis

PASS only when required references have been analyzed sufficiently for the intended Visual DNA extraction.

BLOCK when references are missing, unusable, or insufficient for a claimed conclusion.

## Visual DNA

PASS only when rules are traceable to visual evidence and conflicts have been resolved or documented.

BLOCK when core rules are unsupported or contradictory.

## Art Direction

APPROVE only when the selected concept satisfies strategic requirements and is internally coherent.

REVISE when the concept is viable but the direction is incomplete.

REJECT when no concept satisfies the required criteria.

## Generation

READY only when the specification is complete and the prompt preserves all MUST HAVE, MUST PRESERVE, and MUST NOT HAVE constraints.

BLOCK when the prompt would require the model to invent an unresolved creative decision.

## Quality Control

APPROVE — output satisfies the approved system.

APPROVE WITH MINOR REVISION — no strategic or structural failure.

REVISE — core direction remains valid but execution needs correction.

REGENERATE — fundamental execution failure or wrong creative result.

## Critical Override

Any CRITICAL failure overrides numerical scores and prevents approval.
